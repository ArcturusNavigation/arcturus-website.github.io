## MIT Museum Cart

**Date:** February 2025 to October 2025

This year, Arcturus was fortunate enough to be able to have our very own recurring exhibit at the MIT Museum! Teagan, one of our MechE leads, created an exhibit that was designed to walk learners of all ages through a simplified version of Arcturusâ€™ hull design process, focusing on three main criteria: weight capacity, stability, and ease of movement. Using 3D printed boat parts, miniature weights, stackable blocks, and a tub of water, exhibit visitors test out different designs while optimizing for one category at a time, eventually working towards a boat that balances all three criteria. We ran this exhibit multiple times a semester and were even able to attend an event from the museum's After Dark series, a monthly event with special exhibits.
