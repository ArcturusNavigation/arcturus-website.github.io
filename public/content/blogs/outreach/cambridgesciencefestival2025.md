## Cambridge Science Festival 2025

**Date:** April 12, 2025

For CSF 2025, Teagan prepared an activity that allowed young learners to make and test boats out of pool noodles, popsicle sticks, small weights, construction paper, and stickers. Kids learned about the basic principles of boat stability as they figured out how to design their hull and orient the weighted popsicle stick to allow their boat to float.

We also brought our boat, Fish n' Ships, and walked curious visitors through different aspects of its design and the Roboboat competition. We ran out of supplies a bit before the festival ended, but were able to engage with well over 100 visitors before that point.

It isn't difficult for a student to go their entire academic career without engaging in ocean engineering, not because of a lack of interest, but a lack of exposure and opportunities. A very rewarding part of being on this team is the unique position we're in to provide students with these rare opportunities to participate in this ever-important and constantly evolving field, making CSF one of our favorite days of the year!
